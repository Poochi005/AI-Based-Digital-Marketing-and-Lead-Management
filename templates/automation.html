This is a visual example of a nurturing workflow. It does not send emails or run on a schedule.

New lead nurturing workflow

🎯

Trigger: new lead added

A visitor signs up through the website form or newsletter.

✉️

Send welcome email

Introduce GreenNest and share a first-order offer.

⏳

Wait 3 days

Give the lead time to read and browse.

❓

Condition: did the lead open the email?

Yes
Send product recommendations based on interest.
No
Resend with a new subject line.
⏳

Wait 5 days

Pause before the next touchpoint.

❓

Condition: did the lead click a link?

Yes
Mark as Qualified and notify the sales team.
No
Move to the monthly newsletter list.
📞

Follow-up

Sales contacts qualified leads with a personal message.
